FREQUENTLY ASKED

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A secure engagement begins with well-defined expectations.

Do you test without written authorization?

No. We assess only assets and activities expressly approved by an authorized client through written scope and rules of engagement.

Is testing available remotely?

Yes. We operate remotely when the required access, approvals, and safety arrangements are in place. Some network assessments may need onsite support.

Will we receive a technical report?

Our proposed deliverables include a business summary, verified technical findings, evidence, remediation recommendations, and agreed retesting details.

Can you help verify security fixes?

Yes. Retesting can be included in a defined engagement scope and schedule to confirm whether agreed findings have been addressed.

Are you CERT-In empanelled?

We do not claim CERT-In empanelment. If a project requires a specifically accredited or empanelled assessor, we will clarify that requirement before accepting it.
